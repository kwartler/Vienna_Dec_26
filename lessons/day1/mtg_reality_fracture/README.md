# MTG Reality Fracture functions lab

Data for the Day 1 functions lab: build `openPack()` and `packValue()` from the price list, simulate a box, then open the real box in class and compare.

| File | Contents |
|------|----------|
| `reality_fracture_prices.csv` | 470 singles (cards and tokens): name, printing, condition, rarity, collector number, market price in USD |
| `reality_fracture_sealed_prices.csv` | Market prices for sealed products (packs, displays, bundles) |

Source: [TCGplayer Reality Fracture price guide](https://www.tcgplayer.com/categories/trading-and-collectible-card-games/magic-the-gathering/price-guides/reality-fracture), Near Mint market prices, retrieved November 1, 2026. Prices move daily, so expect the in-class numbers to differ a little.

Each card appears once at its default printing. Foil printings of regular cards are not listed separately; the 44 foil rows are special treatments (showcase, borderless, Fracture Foil and Facet Foil).
