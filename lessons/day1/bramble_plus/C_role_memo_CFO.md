# Bramble+ Memo 2: Chief Financial Officer

*Private. Read alone. Do not pass this memo around.*

Your job on Thursday is to protect revenue and margin. You are deeply skeptical of the CMO's price cut.

**Your numbers**

- About 1.18 million subscribers are on the monthly plan. Cutting €12.99 to €9.99 costs about **€3.5 million of monthly recurring revenue on day one**, roughly €42 million a year, before a single extra subscriber is retained.
- To earn that back, churn would have to fall sharply and stay down for well over a year. You have not seen evidence that it would.
- The ad-supported tier worries you too. A subscriber who trades down from €12.99 to €7.99 plus about €2.50 of ad revenue is worth about €2.50 less every month.
- Content already takes about 45% of revenue. There is no room to absorb a revenue drop.

**Your instinct**

You lean toward **holding course**. To you, the move from 4.1% to 4.6% churn looks like half a point of noise around a normal level, and you suspect the panic is being driven by one dramatic looking chart.

You also take some comfort in how much cleaner the churn reporting has been since May, when your team brought the dashboard in line with investor reporting so that subscribers who pause their account are treated as retained rather than lost. The monthly figures have been steadier since then, which is one more reason you think the recent rise is being overstated. If anything, you would rather cut marketing spend than cut price.

You may change your mind if the evidence persuades you.
