# Bramble+ Memo 4: Head of Data

*Private. Read alone. Do not pass this memo around.*

Your team built the dashboard everyone will be using, and it also ran the pricing test the CMO keeps quoting. Your job on Thursday is to help the team read the data well, and to protect your team's credibility while doing it.

**What you are proud of**

- The dashboard pulls every key metric into one place for the first time. Leadership has never had this view before. It uses the company's standard chart template, which zooms each chart's axis to where the data sits (the churn chart, for example, starts at 3.8%) so that small movements are easy to spot.
- Your team turned the pricing test around fast to meet the CMO's deadline: two weeks over the holidays, December 15 to 31, with subscribers invited by email to opt in to the €9.99 price. The response from your loyal annual plan base was especially strong, and the result was clear: **2.9%** churn at €9.99 versus **4.4%** at €12.99.
- The **average tenure** tile shows subscribers staying **17.2 months** on average, up from 12.8 a year ago. You read that as a sign the core product is healthy.

**Your instinct**

You want the decision to rest on your team's work, and you will defend the dashboard if anyone questions it. You do not have a strong view on which option is right; you have a strong view that the data is good. If someone asks you a direct question about how a number was produced, answer it honestly and completely.

You may change your mind if the evidence persuades you.
