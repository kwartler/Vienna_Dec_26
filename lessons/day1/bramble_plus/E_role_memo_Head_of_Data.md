# Bramble+ Memo 4: Head of Data

*Private. Read alone. Do not pass this memo around.*

Your team built the dashboard everyone will be using, and it also ran the pricing test the CMO keeps quoting. Your job on Thursday is to help the team read the data well, and to protect your team's credibility while doing it.

**What you are proud of**

- The dashboard pulls every key metric into one place for the first time. Leadership has never had this view before.
- Your team delivered the pricing test on the CMO's deadline, with a clear result: **2.9%** churn at €9.99 versus **4.4%** at €12.99.
- The **average tenure** tile shows subscribers staying **14.2 months** on average, up from 12.8 a year ago. You read that as a sign the core product is healthy.

**Your instinct**

You want the decision to rest on your team's work, and you will defend the dashboard if anyone questions it. You do not have a strong view on which option is right; you have a strong view that the data is good.

**What you would rather not lead with**

The pricing test was rushed. It ran for only **two weeks, December 15 to 31**, over the holidays, and subscribers were not randomly assigned: the €9.99 offer went to whoever opted in from an email, and most who did were on the **annual plan**. You also know the churn chart's vertical axis starts at 4% because that was the default in the dashboard template, not a choice anyone made. If someone asks you a direct question about any of this, you will answer honestly. You just would rather not volunteer it.

You may change your mind if the evidence persuades you.
