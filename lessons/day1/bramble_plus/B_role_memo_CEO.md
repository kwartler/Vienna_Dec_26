# Bramble+ Memo 1: Chief Executive Officer

*Private. Read alone. Do not pass this memo around.*

You own Thursday's decision. Your colleagues will advise you, but the board will hold you, not them, accountable for it.

**What you are carrying into this meeting**

- In June, when subscribers peaked at 1.91 million, you told the board that Bramble+ would reach **1.95 million subscribers by year end**. You are now at 1.84 million. The chair has asked, politely, what happened.
- The chair is anxious about the rival's €7.99 ad-supported launch in DACH. The chair has said more than once that "the market moves fast and we need to be seen to move."
- The board wants **one clear story and one number** it can repeat to investors. A recommendation hedged into four options will go badly.

**Your instinct**

You lean toward bold, visible action that gets subscriber numbers growing again, and the CMO's price cut is the most visible option on the table. You are less excited about "hold course," which will feel to the board like doing nothing.

**What you need from your team**

Each of your colleagues has a view, and each may have reasons to emphasize some facts and soften others. Before you decide, ask each of them directly: *what is the strongest argument against your own position?* Then ask the Head of Data how much you should trust the numbers on the dashboard.

You may change your mind if the evidence persuades you. The board will forgive a careful reversal more readily than a confident mistake.
