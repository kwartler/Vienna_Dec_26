# Bramble+: The Churn Decision

*Executive decision challenge, Day 1. No coding required.*

## Your situation

It is Tuesday afternoon. On Thursday morning the board of Bramble+ meets, and the first agenda item is the one everyone in the building is talking about: subscribers are leaving faster than they used to.

You are the leadership team. By the end of this session your group must agree on **one** recommendation and present it to the board as the CEO would. The board does not want a menu of options. It wants a decision, the evidence behind it, and an honest account of what could go wrong.

## The company

Bramble+ is a subscription video streaming service founded in Vienna in 2019. It is known for European drama, film and documentaries, and operates in four regions: DACH, the Nordics, Benelux and Southern Europe.

| Fact (end of October 2026) | Value |
|---|---|
| Paying subscribers | 1.84 million |
| Monthly plan | €12.99 per month, about 64% of subscribers |
| Annual plan | €129 per year (about €10.75 per month), about 36% of subscribers |
| Monthly recurring revenue | about €21.9 million |
| Customer acquisition cost | about €38 per new subscriber |
| Content spend | about 45% of revenue |

Spring 2026 was the best acquisition quarter in the company's history. Subscribers peaked at 1.91 million in June.

## The problem

Monthly churn, the share of subscribers who cancel in a month, averaged **4.1%** from November 2025 through June 2026. In September and October 2026 it was **4.6%**.

At the same time, a large global rival launched a €7.99 ad-supported plan in the DACH region in September. Several board members have forwarded press coverage of it.

The Chief Marketing Officer has proposed an immediate price cut on the monthly plan, from €12.99 to €9.99, and says the company's own data supports it. Not everyone on the leadership team agrees.

## Your options

Choose exactly one.

| Option | What it means | What is known |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Cut the price** | Monthly plan drops to €9.99 for all subscribers, new and existing. Annual plan unchanged. | Can launch in 2 weeks. Affects about 1.18 million monthly subscribers. |
| **2. Launch an ad-supported tier** | Add a €7.99 plan with ads; keep €12.99 ad-free. | Takes about 4 months to build. Ad sales estimates about €2.50 per ad tier subscriber per month. Some current subscribers would trade down. |
| **3. Targeted retention offers** | Offer subscribers flagged as at risk 25% off for 3 months. Everyone else keeps current prices. | Can launch in 3 weeks. Cost depends on how many subscribers are flagged and how well the flag works. |
| **4. Hold course** | Keep prices and plans as they are; proceed with the planned content slate. | No launch cost. The board will ask why doing nothing is safe. |

## What you have

1. **The Bramble+ dashboard**, built by the company's data team: [link provided in class]. Everything you need to make the call is in it. Not everything in it is what it first appears to be.
2. **A private memo for your role**, handed to you at the start of the session. Read it alone. You may argue its position in your group, but do not pass the memo around.

Each group has four roles: **CEO**, **CFO**, **CMO** and **Head of Data**. The CEO owns the final call.

## How the session runs

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 15 min | Read this brief and your private memo |
| 50 min | Work as a group with the dashboard and agree on your recommendation |
| 5 min per group | Present to the board |
| About 20 min | Debrief as a class |

## Your board presentation (5 minutes)

The CEO presents; anyone may answer questions. Cover these five points, in this order:

1. **The decision.** Which option, in one sentence.
2. **The evidence.** No more than three numbers from the dashboard, and why each one matters.
3. **What you are not sure of.** The most important thing the data cannot tell you yet, and how you would find out.
4. **The risk.** What happens to the business if you are wrong, and the signal that would tell you early.
5. **What the board should watch.** The one metric you would report back on at the next meeting.

No slides are required. A whiteboard sketch or a single page is welcome.

## A note on judgment

There is no prize for the most confident presentation. The board rewards groups that read the evidence carefully, say clearly what they do not know, and still make a decision.
